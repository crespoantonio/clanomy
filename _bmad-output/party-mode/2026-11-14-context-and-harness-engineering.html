💬 Key Turning Points from the Round-Table
🏗️
Winston (System Architect)
Prompt engineering was microscopic—tweaking adjectives in a string. Modern AI systems split cleanly into Context Engineering (what enters short-term memory) and Harness Engineering (the deterministic environment that constrains and verifies execution).
💻
Amelia (Senior Software Engineer)
Look at src/services/query/synthesizer.py#L194. If a family has 450 transactions and asks for their 6-month grocery spend, we dumped all 450 items into the prompt! That overflows local Ollama 4k windows and blows cloud API budgets. We need hierarchical budgeting: Top 5 exemplars, exact pre-calculated math aggregates.
👤
Tony (Project Lead)
"Before we move to harness, have we added a time frame before actually reviewing previous messages? If a person sends a message, and then 3 hours later sends another, for sure those two are not related, did we consider that?"
📋
John (Product Manager)
Tony, that is an absolute bullseye. Without an inactivity TTL, messages sent hours or days apart suffer from Temporal Anchoring Bias. The model assumes you are still discussing the morning topic when you send an afternoon note.
🧪
Murat (Master Test Architect)
And look at the harness: when Gemini hits its 3-iteration limit or encounters a rate limit, the bot must never respond with blank text or crash. The Fallback Circuit Breaker catches it and falls back to our verified DecoupledQueryPipeline. 728 tests green prove the architecture holds!
