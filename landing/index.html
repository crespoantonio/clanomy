Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Clanomy is committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidentiality of your personal financial records.

1. Information We Collect

  • Telegram Identity: User ID, Chat ID, and display name provided by Telegram.
  • Bookkeeping Data: Financial concepts, amounts, categories, and dates you choose to log.
  • Billing Metadata: Subscription status, plan tier, and customer ID reference. We never see or store your raw payment card numbers. All payment processing is handled securely by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors.

2. Zero-Knowledge Field Encryption

Clanomy implements application-level AES-256 URL-safe Fernet encryption. All transaction amounts and merchant descriptions are encrypted prior to database persistence. Server-side database snapshots cannot read your financial records in plaintext.

3. How We Use Data

Your data is used strictly to provide the bookkeeping, query aggregation, and Notion synchronization requested by you. We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Integrations

  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing, invoicing, and subscription management.
  • Notion: When enabled by you, transactions are transmitted via TLS 1.3 to your personal Notion database.
  • AI Inference & Zero Training: Transcription and extraction are executed through secured, zero-data-retention commercial developer AI endpoints solely for the transient duration of the request. Customer data, voice recordings, and financial prompts are strictly never retained or used to train machine learning models.

5. Data Deletion Rights

You may permanently delete your workspace, transactions, and linked credentials at any time by executing the /delete_account command or by writing to support@clanomy.com.
